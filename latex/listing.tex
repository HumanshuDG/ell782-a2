{\Large\textbf{Program Listing -- Washing-Machine Control Program}}\\[2pt]
\project\ -- \course
\end{center}

Source: \texttt{program.asm}. ROM image: \texttt{program.hex} (Logisim \texttt{v2.0 raw} format, one 16-bit word per address,
load with \emph{right-click ROM $\rightarrow$ Load Image}). The same words are already stored in the ROM of
\texttt{washing\_pipeline.circ}. The encoding is mechanical: \textbf{R} = \texttt{op$\ll$12 | ra$\ll$9 | rb$\ll$6},
\textbf{I} = \texttt{op$\ll$12 | ra$\ll$9 | imm8}, \textbf{N} = \texttt{op$\ll$12}
(\texttt{tools/asm.py} only automates this table look-up). The program has 180 words (\hex{00}--\hex{B3}). Unused ROM words are \hex{0000} (\texttt{NOP}).

DISPLAY low nibble: 1 idle, 2 lock/check, 3 fill, 4 wash, 5 drain, 6 spin, 7 stopping, 8 done;
